# Booking a free Ads Optimization Review

**Audience:** Support, Multi-Location specialists, account managers. **Type:** internal.

## What it is
A 30-minute session with a Yelp Ads specialist to review targeting, budgets and lead performance by location, and to set launch budgets for new locations.

## When to offer it
- A customer is opening new locations and wants to plan launch spend.
- A location's cost per lead is more than 1.5x the account average.
- A customer questions the value they're getting before a renewal.

## Who books it
- Support agents: suggest it to the account manager (it's included in the AE alert for multi-location accounts). Don't promise a call time to the customer.
- Account managers: book it directly with the customer and invite an Ads specialist.

## What to prepare
- Spend, leads and cost per lead for each location (last 30 and 90 days).
- Planned openings and dates.
- Renewal date and current annual ad spend.
