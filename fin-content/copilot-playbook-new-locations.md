# Options for multi-location customers opening new locations or redirecting spend

**Audience:** Support and Multi-Location specialists. **Type:** internal (Copilot). **Effective:** October 2026.

Use this when a multi-location customer is opening new locations, wants to move or redirect existing spend to new locations, or wants to see new locations perform before investing more.

## What options are available?
Offer these, in this order:

1. **New Location Launch Program (new).** Customers with **10 or more active locations** get **$500 in ad credit for each new location** in its first 60 days. Example: a 12-location customer opening 4 new locations gets **$2,000** in launch credit ($500 x 4). This lets them see performance before adding budget, and it's the best fit when the customer says new locations don't always perform as expected.
2. **Temporary budget shift.** Move **up to 20%** of an existing location's monthly budget to a new location for **90 days**. Then both budgets switch back automatically. Total monthly spend doesn't change.
3. **Free Ads Optimization Review (for the account manager).** Suggest the account manager books a review to set launch budgets for the new locations.

**Before you reply, check:** the customer's renewal date. If the renewal is within 90 days, flag it for the account manager.

## Reply to the customer (template)
"Congratulations on the new locations! Because you have [N] active locations, you qualify for our New Location Launch Program: $500 in ad credit for each new location in its first 60 days. For your [X] new locations, that's $[500 x X] to see how they perform before you invest more. If you'd also like to move some existing budget, you can shift up to 20% of a location's budget to a new one for 90 days. Want me to enroll you in the launch program?"

## After the customer says yes (confirmation template)
"Done, [first name]. You're enrolled in the New Location Launch Program. The $500 credit is applied automatically to each new location when its Yelp page goes live, for its first 60 days. You can compare the new locations' leads side by side in Yelp for Business. Your account manager will reach out to plan launch budgets."

## How to enroll
In the Service Console, open **Support tools** on the conversation, choose **Enroll in New Location Launch Program**, leave Location blank, and click **Confirm**.

## Common questions
- **Does a 12-location account qualify?** Yes. 10+ active locations qualifies.
- **Is there a cap on new locations?** No. $500 for each new location.
- **Can the credit be combined with a budget shift?** Yes.
- **When does the credit apply?** When each new location's Yelp page goes live, for its first 60 days.
