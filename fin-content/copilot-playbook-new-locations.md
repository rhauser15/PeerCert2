# Options for multi-location customers opening new locations or redirecting spend

**Audience:** Support and Multi-Location specialists. **Type:** internal (Copilot). **Effective:** October 2026.

Use this when a multi-location customer is opening new locations, wants to move or redirect existing spend to new locations, wants to see new locations perform before investing more, or is unsure about the value they're getting.

## What options are available?
Support can offer three options. Record the one the customer accepts in **Support tools** (Location: *Entire account* or one location).

1. **New Location Launch Program ($500 per new location).** Customers with **10 or more active locations** get **$500 in ad credit for each new location** in its first 60 days. Example: a 12-location customer opening 4 new locations gets **$2,000** in launch credit ($500 x 4). Best fit when the customer is opening locations and wants to see performance before investing more. In Support tools: *Enroll account in New Location Launch Program*, Location *Entire account*.
2. **Pause ad spending for 90 days, no fee.** Pause one location, or the entire account, for up to 90 days with no fee. The page, reviews and ad history stay live, and ads restart automatically after 90 days. Best fit when a location needs a breather, for example while the customer focuses budget on a launch. In Support tools: *Pause ad spending for 90 days, no fee*, then pick the location or *Entire account*.
3. **$1,000 retention offer across all locations.** A one-time **$1,000 ad credit**, split across all of the account's locations. Best fit when the customer is questioning value or considering reducing spend. In Support tools: *Apply $1,000 retention offer across all locations*.

**Recommended for a customer opening new locations who wants to see performance first:** option 1.

## Ads Optimization Review (account manager, not a Support tools option)
Support doesn't book this. When the case is resolved, the **account manager is alerted automatically** for multi-location accounts with **$50K+ annual ad spend** and a churn or expansion signal from Fin. The account manager then offers a free Ads Optimization Review to set launch budgets and plan the renewal. Tell the customer their account manager will reach out to plan launch budgets.

**Before you reply, check:** the customer's renewal date. If it's within 90 days, mention that the account manager will also review the renewal.

## Reply to the customer (template)
"Congratulations on the new locations! Because you have [N] active locations, you qualify for our New Location Launch Program: $500 in ad credit for each new location in its first 60 days. For your [X] new locations, that's $[500 x X] to see how they perform before you invest more. Want me to enroll your account?"

## After the customer says yes (confirmation template)
"Done, [first name]. Your account is enrolled in the New Location Launch Program. The $500 credit is applied automatically to each new location when its Yelp page goes live, for its first 60 days. You can compare the new locations' leads side by side in Yelp for Business. Your account manager will reach out to plan launch budgets together."

## Common questions
- **Does a 12-location account qualify for the launch program?** Yes. 10+ active locations qualifies.
- **Is there a cap on new locations?** No. $500 for each new location.
- **Can the pause apply to every location?** Yes. Choose *Entire account*.
- **Can a customer get the launch program and the retention offer?** Use one offer per conversation. Pick the one that fits, and the account manager can review more options.
