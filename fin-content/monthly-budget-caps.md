# How monthly budget caps work

**Type:** public.

Each location has its own monthly ad budget cap. When a location reaches its cap, its ads pause automatically until the cap is raised or the next month starts on the 1st.

- You're only charged for what a location actually spends, up to its cap.
- You can raise a location's cap any time in Yelp for Business or by asking in the Messenger. Ads restart within about 15 minutes.
- Busy locations can hit their cap early in the month. If that happens often, consider a higher cap or moving budget from a slower location.
