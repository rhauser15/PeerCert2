# Move budget between your locations

**Type:** public (customers can see this).

Multi-location businesses can temporarily move part of an existing location's monthly ad budget to another location on the same account, for example to give a new location a boost while it launches.

## How it works
- You can move **up to 20%** of a location's monthly budget cap to another location.
- A temporary move lasts **90 days**, then both budgets switch back automatically.
- Your total monthly spend across locations doesn't change.
- Moves take effect within 15 minutes and are prorated for the current month.

## How to request a move
- Ask in the Yelp for Business Messenger ("move budget to my new location"), or
- Contact your Multi-Location specialist or account manager.

## Good to know
- New locations need a live Yelp page before ads can run.
- If you're opening several locations, ask about the **New Location Launch Program** for additional launch credit.
