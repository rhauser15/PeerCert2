# Multi-Location Retention Policy (10+ locations)

**Audience:** Support and Multi-Location specialists. **Type:** internal.

Use these options before a multi-location customer cancels, reduces spend, or when they question the value they're getting. Record the accepted option in **Support tools** on the conversation.

## Options Support can offer
- **Pause ad spending for 90 days, no fee:** one location or the entire account. The page, reviews and ad history stay live, and ads restart automatically.
- **$1,000 retention offer across all locations:** a one-time $1,000 ad credit split across the account's locations.
- **New Location Launch Program:** for customers opening new locations, $500 credit per new location (see that article).

## Approvals
- One offer per conversation. Anything beyond these needs CS manager approval.
- Always record the accepted offer in Support tools so it's on the case.

## After the conversation
Mark the case resolved. For multi-location accounts with $50K+ annual ad spend and a churn or expansion signal from Fin, the account manager is alerted automatically in Slack and follows up with a free Ads Optimization Review. Support doesn't book the review.
