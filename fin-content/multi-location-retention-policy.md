# Multi-Location Retention Policy (10+ locations)

**Audience:** Support and Multi-Location specialists. **Type:** internal.

Use these options before a multi-location customer cancels or reduces spend.

## Options
- **Pause instead of cancel:** pause a location's ads for up to 90 days with no fee. The page, reviews and ad history stay live.
- **Performance credit:** if a location's cost per lead is more than 2x the account's other locations, offer up to 20% of its monthly budget as credit for 2 months.
- **Free Ads Optimization Review:** retune targeting with a specialist.
- **New Location Launch Program:** for customers opening new locations, $500 credit per new location (see that article).

## Approvals
- Credits above $1,000 a month need CS manager approval.
- Always record the accepted offer in Support tools so it's on the case.

## After the conversation
Mark the case resolved. For multi-location accounts with $50K+ annual ad spend and a churn or growth signal, the account manager is alerted automatically in Slack.
