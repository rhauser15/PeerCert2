Procedure: Ad not showing

Fin follows these steps when a customer asks why an ad isn't running.

Look up the account

  1. Use Get ad account to find the customer's locations, budget caps and budget status in Salesforce.
  2. If the customer names a location, find it in the results. Otherwise ask which location they mean.

Explain and fix

  1. IFthe location's budget status is paused
budget_status is "Paused - Budget Cap Reached"
  1. Tell the customer when the ad stopped and that it reached its monthly cap. Say when the cap resets.
  2. Offer to raise the cap. Suggest a cap about a third higher, and let the customer choose.
  3. When the customer confirms, Use Update budget cap for that location, then confirm the new cap and when ads resume.
  1. ELSEcheck targeting and ad approval status, and explain what's holding the ad.

Hand off when it's bigger than one ad

  1. If the customer wants to cancel or pause a location, or mentions opening a new one, hand off to the Multi-Location team.
  2. Before handing off, set Churn risk Expansion signal Sentiment and write a summary, so the customer doesn't have to repeat anything.
